# Lò Luyện Đan (`lo-editor`)

## Identity

Equipment editor for the player's Lò. 9 slots: Đỉnh, Thân, Đế, Quai L/R, Nắp, Khắc Phù, Đồ Sương L/R, Dây Phong Ấn. Click slot to see details, drag from stash to swap. Central furnace visualization.

## Layout principles

- Central: Lò visualization with labeled slots (drag-to-change)
- Below: selected slot detail panel (implicit, prefix, suffix, cấp, reroll/seal actions)
- Bottom: Lò stats summary (cấp, slots used, power score)
- Footer: stash preview for quick swap
- Background: dim alchemist's workshop interior, single ornate furnace on stone workbench
