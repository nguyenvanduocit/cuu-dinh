# Đan Đạo Môn (`dan-dao-sect`)

## Identity

Player's home lineage. Founded by Cao Tổ ~800 years ago. Now reduced to Sư Phụ, a few scattered disciples, and player.

## Membership

Sư Phụ (leader), Đan Sư Hắc Bạch (crafting), player (newest disciple). Historically: Cao Tổ (founder), 7 đan sư mạnh nhất (dead).

## Relations

Neutral with Đạo Quán Cờ Bạc Hội. Antagonistic to Tâm Ma race. Watched by Tổ Sư Bất Tử's hidden line.

## Notes

- Philosophy: "Linh đan = cứu nhân thế. Tu hành = phụng sự."
