# Hỗn Nguyên Tâm Ma Vương (`primordial-corruption-king-lore`)

## Identity

The failed Cửu Chuyển Kim Đan wearing the shape of humanity's collective desire. Born 800 years ago from the seventh turning onward.

## Story

Carries the voices of the seven dead đan sư and the severed half-soul of Cao Tổ. Cao Tổ partially sealed him. Seal weakening in player's time. Goal: finish the ninth turning by entering player's Lò and becoming a 'perfect' linh đan that no longer separates human desire from heavenly law. Does not want to destroy world with fire — wants to prove all cultivation is ambition with a pretty name.
