# Cửu Chuyển Kim Đan (`nine-turn-golden-elixir`)

## Identity

The mythical perfect elixir. Original attempt 800 years ago failed and became Hỗn Nguyên Tâm Ma Vương.

## Story

Modern Cửu Chuyển Đan currency is only a fragmentary imitation: one safe-ish 'turn', not the full forbidden ninth turning. Full Cửu Chuyển Kim Đan is a myth, a warning, and a mirror-tier endgame object only after campaign truth is known.
