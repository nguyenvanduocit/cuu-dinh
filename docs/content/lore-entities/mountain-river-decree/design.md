# Sơn Hà Đồ Lệnh (`mountain-river-decree`)

## Identity

Map device. Opens portals to 'spaces that have ever existed'. Created by Tổ Sư Bất Tử (Cảnh Hữu Chân Nhân) 8000 years ago.

## Story

Originally 1 single artifact. Broken into multiple shards after Cửu Chuyển thất bại, one given to each Sơ Tổ. Player reassembles complete version at end of Act 5. Unlocks Atlas (Sơn Hà Đồ = game's endgame map).
