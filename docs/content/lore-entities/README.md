# `lore-entities/` — Lore Artifacts & Historical Figures

> 7 entities: Cao Tổ, Phong Ấn Cao Tổ, Cửu Chuyển Kim Đan, Lò Luyện Đan Tổ Truyền, Lò Thần, Sơn Hà Đồ Lệnh, Hỗn Nguyên Tâm Ma Vương.

## Design rationale

Lore entities are the mythological foundation of Đan Kiếp. They are not gameplay objects — they are narrative anchors that give meaning to the player's journey. The backbone narrative centres on the failed Cửu Chuyển Kim Đan attempt 800 years ago, the trận Đan Kiếp that followed, and the player's endgame quest to complete what Cao Tổ could not.

## Entity layout

```
lore-entities/<id>/
├── design.md   # required — lore significance, location, relationship to player
└── lore.md     # optional — extended mythological narrative
```
