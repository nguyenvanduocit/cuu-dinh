# `lore-entities/` — Lore Artifacts & Historical Figures

> 16 entities: 9 dinh (cauldrons) + 7 dan su (alchemists) + Cao To + Lo Than.

## Design rationale

Lore entities are the mythological foundation of Cuu Dinh. They are not gameplay objects — they are narrative anchors that give meaning to the player's journey. The 9 dinh are the game's namesake and the endgame quest objective.

## Entity layout

```
lore-entities/<id>/
├── design.md   # required — lore significance, location, relationship to player
└── lore.md     # optional — extended mythological narrative
```
