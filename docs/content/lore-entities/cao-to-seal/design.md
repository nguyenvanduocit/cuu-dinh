# Phong Ấn Cao Tổ (`cao-to-seal`)

## Identity

The seal binding Tâm Ma Vương under Hồn Nguyên Điện. 800 years old, weakening.

## Story

Maintained by 7 successive Sư Phụ generations. Sư Phụ current weakened it further by trying to call back his daughters. Player's quest = renew + reinforce the seal.
