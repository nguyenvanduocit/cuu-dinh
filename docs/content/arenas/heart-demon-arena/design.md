# Tâm Ma Trận Địa (`heart-demon-arena`)

## Identity

Arena cho boss Tâm Ma (T6-T13). Tím triều đậm, aura sương khói dày đặc, bóng người mang mặt nạ kinh hoàng lẩn khuất ở rìa. Oppressive, whispering dread, claustrophobic. Player phải chiến đấu với phản chiếu ham muốn của chính mình.

Xuất hiện trong Act 4 boss và atlas T6-T13 encounters.

## Boss

Hosts all `heart-demon` type bosses:
- `../../bosses/heart-demon-dai-sat/` — Act 4 (T10)
- `../../bosses/heart-demon-secret-realm/` — Đan Pháp Tâm Ma special (T11+)
- `../../bosses/underworld-heart-demon-lord/` — Diêm Phủ region (T13)

Also hosts campaign final (multi-type):
- `../../bosses/primordial-corruption-king/` — Act 5 (T13, hostsBoss override)

## Geometry (combat layout)

Claustrophobic space. Edges shrouded in darkness — effective board smaller than actual. Shadow silhouettes at edges can block linh lực paths. Smoky aura reduces visibility progressively in later phases.

Art direction ref: `art-direction.md:164-168` — Dark tím triều base, smoky aura, human-shaped silhouettes with horrific masks, whispers.

## Visual signature

Deep twilight purple (#4A1942) darkness, thick smoky aura, faint human-shaped shadow silhouettes wearing horrific opera masks lurking at the edges. Oppressive whispering dread, claustrophobic composition. Twilight purple and ink black palette with sickly accents.
