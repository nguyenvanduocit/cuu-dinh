# Dị Tượng Trận Địa (`anomaly-arena`)

## Identity

Arena cho boss Dị Tượng (T1-T8). Bất đối xứng, glitch, hỗn loạn. Kiến trúc không ổn định, địa hình tạo từ các mảnh nguyên tố kết hợp sai — lửa/nước/mộc/kim/thổ fused wrongly. Màu sắc chuyển biến không đoán trước. Reality distortion.

Xuất hiện trong Act bosses 1-3 và atlas T1-T8 encounters.

## Boss

Hosts all `anomaly` type bosses:
- `../../bosses/heart-demon-son-moc/` — Act 1 (T2)
- `../../bosses/anomaly-wuxing/` — Act 2 (T5)
- `../../bosses/heart-demon-hoa-son/` — Act 3 (T8)

## Geometry (combat layout)

Asymmetric layout with unstable terrain. Anchor points shift subtly between phases. Board edges irregular (not rectangular). Element fragments create visual noise — player must focus on linh đan through chaos.

Art direction ref: `art-direction.md:159-162` — Asymmetric, glitchy, hỗn loạn; colors shift unpredictably; made of multiple element fragments.

## Visual signature

Chaotic anomaly battle arena, asymmetric glitching architecture, unstable terrain of mismatched elemental fragments — bits of fire, water, wood, metal and earth fused wrongly together. Colors shifting unpredictably, reality distortion, unsettling broken composition. Full six-color palette clashing.
