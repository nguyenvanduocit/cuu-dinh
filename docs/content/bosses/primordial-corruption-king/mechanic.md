# Hỗn Nguyên Tâm Ma Vương — Mechanics

Extracted from `../README.md`.

**Role**: Campaign climax. Reveals Cửu Chuyển thất bại, tests whether player can refine desire without repeating Cao Tổ's mistake. Drops Sơn Hà Đồ Lệnh.

**Phases**: 4

**Phase 1: Awakening** (100% → 80%)
- Form: shadowy giant made of broken đan shells and seven dead đan-sư masks, no specific element
- **Multi-element Volley** (5s cooldown): 4 lực of different elements, random.
- **Aura**: Tâm ma orbs spawn passively, 1 per 3s.
- **Whisper counter**: Each 20% quality gained triggers a faint count: "Nhất chuyển... Nhị chuyển..." up to "Bát chuyển". Pure foreshadowing, no mechanical damage yet.
- **Music**: low cello, ominous.

**Phase 2: Manipulation** (80% → 50%)
- Form: calm human shape, then shifts into Sư Phụ's appearance (visual cruelty)
- **Cửu Chuyển reveal dialogue**: Boss reveals it was born from the failed Cửu Chuyển Kim Đan, not from random evil.
- **Mirror Sư Phụ dialogue**: "Con, ngừng lại đi. Thầy đã thử cứu người thân bằng Lò này." — text appears on screen, ignore (illusion).
- **Tâm Ma Spawn**: 1 mini-Tâm Ma per 10s. Each must be dispelled.
- **Linh Đan Shift Threat**: Boss attempts to FORCE linh đan element to shift to neutral (kill build identity). Player must use Cổ vật or specific affix to resist.
- **Greed read**: If player entered with high-risk mods, boss spawns one extra reward-looking orb. Hitting it gives +20 quality and -25 stability. It is intentionally tempting.

**Phase 3: True Form** (50% → 20%)
- Form: 7-faced demon, each face one dead đan sư's desire. Only 4 faces active in campaign version; postgame can use all 7.
- **Element Symphony**: Attacks rotate through 4 faces. Each face = element burst. Player must time linh khí placement carefully.
- **Sư Phụ joins**: NPC Sư Phụ appears, joins fight. Adds +30% intensity. (Story moment.)
- **Confession trigger**: At 35%, Sư Phụ confesses he widened the seal by trying to call back his daughters. Boss gains +50% manipulation intensity for 10s. Player loses the Sư Phụ buff during this window, then regains it if stability stays above 30.

**Phase 4: Sacrifice** (20% → 0%)
- **Sư Phụ sacrifices himself**: dies on-screen, his energy fuses into player's Lò temporarily (+200% intensity, +50 stability for last phase).
- **Final Stand**: Boss goes berserk, all attacks max intensity.
- **Ninth Turning Reversal**: Player must complete a special refinement:
  - Push linh đan to target quality.
  - Keep stability ≥50 when final hit lands.
  - Avoid "temptation orbs" that offer huge quality but heavy stability loss.
  - If stability drops below 50 at final hit, boss does not wipe; it resets Phase 4 once and says: "Lại là Cửu Chuyển cũ."
- **Empty Elixir**: Successful completion creates a hollow linh đan that absorbs the boss without feeding it. This is the story/mechanic proof that player did not repeat Cao Tổ's mistake.

**Reward**:
- Sơn Hà Đồ Lệnh (map device — unlocks Atlas)
- 1 guaranteed Cổ vật + 1 Cổ vật chance
- 100,000 gold
- +100,000 XP
- Story unlocks: Endgame era begins.
